# Smart Expense & Budget Analyzer

## Problem Statement

Students often find it difficult to keep track of their daily expenses and understand their spending patterns. Expenses are frequently recorded manually or not tracked at all, making it difficult to monitor budgets, identify unnecessary spending, and plan monthly expenses effectively.

The Smart Expense & Budget Analyzer is a Python-based command-line application designed to help users record, manage, analyze, and monitor their personal expenses. The system allows users to maintain expense records, categorize transactions, set monthly and category-wise budgets, analyze spending patterns, and generate financial reports.

The system provides insights such as total spending, category-wise spending, average daily expenditure, budget utilization, and spending alerts.

## Scope

The project focuses on personal expense and budget management through a command-line interface.

The system supports:

* Adding, viewing, updating, and deleting expenses
* Searching and filtering expenses
* Categorizing expenses
* Setting monthly budgets
* Setting category-wise budgets
* Analyzing spending patterns
* Generating spending insights
* Generating financial reports
* Exporting category-wise reports to CSV
* Input validation and error handling

The project does not provide professional financial or investment advice.

## Target Users

* College students
* Individuals tracking personal expenses
* Users who want to monitor monthly budgets
* Users interested in understanding their spending patterns
