# Smart Expense & Budget Analyzer

## Problem Statement

Students often find it difficult to keep track of their daily expenses and understand their spending patterns. Expenses are frequently recorded manually or not tracked at all, making it difficult to monitor budgets, identify unnecessary spending, and plan monthly expenses effectively.

The Smart Expense & Budget Analyzer is a Python-based command-line application designed to help users record, manage, analyze, and monitor their personal expenses. The system allows users to maintain expense records, categorize transactions, set monthly and category-wise budgets, analyze spending patterns, and generate financial reports.

The system will provide meaningful insights such as total spending, category-wise spending, average daily expenditure, budget utilization, and spending alerts. The application will use modular Python programming, file-based data storage, input validation, exception handling, and automated testing.

## Scope

The project focuses on personal expense and budget management through a command-line interface. It will support expense recording, expense modification, expense deletion, expense searching, budget management, spending analytics, and report generation.

The project does not provide professional financial or investment advice. Its purpose is to help users understand and organize their recorded spending data.

## Target Users

* College students
* Individuals who want to track personal expenses
* Users who want to monitor monthly budgets
* Users who want to understand their spending patterns

## High-Level Features

1. Expense Management
2. Budget Management
3. Expense Analytics
4. Spending Pattern Analysis
5. Budget Alerts
6. Financial Report Generation
7. CSV-based Data Storage
8. Input Validation and Error Handling
9. Automated Testing

## Expected Outcome

The completed system will provide a simple command-line interface through which users can manage expenses, monitor budgets, analyze spending behavior, and generate reports.
